Building a stronger entrepreneurial community

GECG is a platform that brings Gabonese entrepreneurs together, strengthens their businesses and creates meaningful connections in Ghana's business ecosystem.

Founded in 2020, the club builds on a community of Gabonese entrepreneurs with experience in business, networking and community initiatives.

Our story

Far from home, we were many, talented and ambitious, but scattered. One woman saw that our strength would come from uniting.

In 2020, Mrs. Ghislaine looked around and asked a simple question: what if we became one? A team with heart was ready to carry the idea, and the Gabonese Entrepreneurs Club was born: a home and a family for Gabonese builders on Ghanaian soil.

Leaders such as Mr. Ruben Tomo and Sir Obame carried the torch. Mrs. Sabrina then led the club from 2021 to 2026, growing it despite the challenges.

A new chapter

Today the club is led by President Dancy Odjinobo, with a concrete and ambitious plan: turn GECG from a community into an engine of opportunity, success and impact for every Gabonese entrepreneur in Ghana.

Even far from Gabon, Gabon remains united. Welcome home.
